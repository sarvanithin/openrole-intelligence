# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the OpenRole Intelligence community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use the project; and
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
publishing private information, or conduct that could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub security advisory when possible, or
ask a maintainer to move a public discussion to a private channel. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that
do not align with this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
