Public methodology

Evidence you can challenge.

OpenRole reports signals, provenance, and freshness. It never promises that a company will sponsor a particular applicant.

Evidence tiers

A requires an explicit immigration, visa, or H-1B sponsorship offer tied to the current role. E is an explicit current-posting denial and overrides every other signal. B and C require reviewed employer-history links. D means the evidence is missing, conditional, ambiguous, conflicting, or unresolved.

Government data limits

A certified DOL LCA is an employer filing and prerequisite—not proof that a petition was filed or approved, and not proof that a current opening offers sponsorship. Worker positions are requested positions, not unique hires.

Job freshness

Jobs show when they were last observed at the employer source. A job closes only after two complete successful source manifests omit it. Failed, partial, blocked, or anomalously empty runs cannot close jobs.

United States scope

The public explorer includes only roles whose source location clearly identifies one of the 50 U.S. states or Washington, DC. Ambiguous remote, worldwide, non-U.S., conflicting, and U.S.-territory-only locations are retained for source reconciliation but fail closed and are not published.

Privacy

The public beta has no applicant accounts, résumé uploads, tracking pixels, advertising profiles, or application automation. Standard infrastructure access logs may temporarily record IP address, request path, and user agent for reliability and abuse prevention.

Corrections

Every assessment carries a rule version and source metadata. Operators should publish a contact address for corrections and remove or amend records when credible evidence shows an error.
